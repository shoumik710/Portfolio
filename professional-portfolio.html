The newsroom — editorial work

Professional Portfolio

A newsroom teaches you that words carry responsibility, and that every sentence has a purpose, a deadline, and a reader waiting on the other side. My experience in journalism has taught me to work with accuracy, clarity, speed, and attention to detail.

As a sub editor, much of the work happens quietly. Editing a lead, checking facts, refining language, shaping headlines, translating stories, and making sure every detail is clear before publication. Sometimes the work carries my name. Often it does not. Both are equally important.

For me, journalism is not only about reporting what happened. It is about finding the clearest and most responsible way to tell the story.
