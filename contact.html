Get in touch

Much of my work begins with a curiosity about words, people, and ideas. I love writing books, translating books, and exploring the stories that connect us. I work across communication, public relations, journalism, development, and branding, with a particular interest in meaningful communication and creative storytelling.

If you have a book that needs translation, a story that needs shaping, a communication challenge, or an idea worth discussing, I would be glad to hear from you. You can write to me in Bangla or English, whichever feels more natural. Writing, translation, journalism, PR, communication, development, branding, or simply a good conversation, all are welcome.
