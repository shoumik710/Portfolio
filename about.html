About Me

I am a dedicated student of Media Studies and Journalism with a major in Public Relations and a minor in Sustainable Development. I have a strong interest in strategic communication, reputation management, and the role of media in shaping public perception. My academic training has equipped me with skills in media analysis, campaign planning, audience engagement and news writting.

I am particularly passionate about integrating communication strategies with sustainable development goals, aiming to create meaningful social impact through responsible and ethical public relations practices. I am eager to apply my knowledge in real-world settings, contribute to organizational growth, and develop innovative communication solutions in a dynamic professional environment.

Location Dhaka, Bangladesh

Most people meet me twice before they realise it's the same person: once on a spine in a Dhaka bookshop, where the name reads ইকরাম মাহমুদ, and once in a university corridor with a recorder in the bag.

Translating came first — Welcome to the Hyunam-dong Bookshop, Twelfth Fail, The Four Agreements. Seventeen of them now, on one principle: a reader in Bangla shouldn't have to learn English first to get the good sentence. Then my own — seven written, Four edited for other authors. Twenty nine titles across eleven publishing houses, most of it done at night, between semesters.

The daylight half is louder. I took over operations at ULAB Radio CampBuzz in Spring 2023, went on air that summer as a Radio Jockey, and by 2024 was running the station's output as Programme Head. Around it: PR4U since Fall 2022, four semesters coordinating the Trashion Show, nine documentary and video works, and training in the unglamorous parts — fact-checking, investigative reporting, media literacy.

From outside it looks like two careers. From inside it's one. You carry something true across a distance, and you try not to break it on the way.
